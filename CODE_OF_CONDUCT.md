# Code of conduct

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, and assume good faith.
- Give and accept feedback graciously.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users, including safety around
  physical robots.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other
contributions that violate this code, and may ban contributors for behavior they
judge inappropriate. To report a problem, open a private security advisory on this
repository's GitHub "Security" tab and mark it as a conduct report.
