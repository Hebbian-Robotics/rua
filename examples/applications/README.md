# Example applications

These small projects use a physical SO-101. They share the
[Python example environment](../python/README.md).

- [Inspect Blocks](inspect-blocks/README.md) looks at blocks with a wrist camera
  and answers yes or no with a nod or shake.
- [Image Q&A](image-qa/README.md) looks at an image on a display, searches the
  web, and answers yes or no with a gesture.
